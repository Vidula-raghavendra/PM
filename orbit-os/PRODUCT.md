## Platform

web

## Users

Professionals and teams who manage client projects and need to track deliverables, payments, and time in one place. Includes freelancers, consultants, designers, developers, agencies, and small firms. Not limited to a specific industry. Indian market focus (INR currency default) but not India-exclusive.

## Product Purpose

Orbit OS is a project management workspace that ties project delivery to financial outcomes. It exists because most PM tools separate project tracking from payment tracking, forcing professionals to juggle spreadsheets for finances alongside their project tool. Success means a user can see their total revenue, active projects, hours logged, and next task in one view, and never lose track of what a client owes or what a collaborator's share is.

## Positioning

Project management with built-in milestone payments and team revenue splits. Unlike Asana/Trello/Notion, Orbit OS treats money as a first-class object: every project phase has an associated payment amount, status (pending/paid/overdue), and revenue split percentages per collaborator.

## Operating Context

Users create projects with client details, budgets, and deadlines. Projects are broken into milestones (phases) with payment amounts and checklists. Collaborators are invited by email and assigned revenue split percentages. Users log time against projects with a built-in timer. Calendar shows project deadlines, milestones, and scheduled meetings. Goals track personal objectives separate from client work. Contracts and invoices are uploaded and stored per project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-phase project creation with 5-step wizard (details, phases, team, budget/split, review)
- Milestone payment tracking with pending/paid/overdue states
- Collaborator invitations by email with role and split percentage
- Built-in time tracker with start/stop timer
- Monthly calendar with color-coded events (project, milestone, task, event types)
- Meeting scheduling with external link support and attendee management
- Goal tracking (active/achieved)
- Contract and document storage via Supabase Storage
- Real-time notification system (invitations, meetings, general)
- Onboarding flow (phone, gender, industry/sector, purpose)
- Auth: email + password, auto-confirmed signup
- Currently free (lifetime beta access, ₹0)
- No mobile app, no offline support, no integrations with external tools yet

## Product Principles

1. Money is a first-class object -- every project phase ties to a payment.
2. Clarity over features -- show what matters (revenue, deadlines, next task) without clutter.
3. Team transparency -- collaborators see their role, split, and shared project status.
4. Low friction -- invite by email, one-click timer, minimal onboarding.
5. Professional, not enterprise -- built for people who do the work, not managers of managers.
